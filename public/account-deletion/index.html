Delete your MonsterDialer account

You can request deletion of your MonsterDialer account and the data associated with it, including your Firebase Authentication account.

To submit a request:

  • Email us from the Google account used with MonsterDialer.
  • Use the subject “MonsterDialer account deletion”.
  • Include the email address associated with your MonsterDialer account.

Send your request to monsterdialerapp@gmail.com.

After verifying the request, we will delete the Firebase Authentication account and the associated MonsterDialer data, such as saved preferences and profile data, within 30 days. We may retain information only where required by law or for legitimate security and fraud-prevention purposes, for the period required by law.
